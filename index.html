Welcome

Hey Ladies! 💕

I am SO excited to spend my birthday with each of you!

I haven't done a big birthday celebration in about half a decade, so apparently I've decided to make up for lost time.

Now, I KNOW December feels far away. Like girl...it is September. BUT because my birthday is literally 9 days before Christmas, Philly is already in full holiday mode that weekend, and restaurants, events, hotels, etc. start booking up early.

So Birthday Chidi has had to become Project Manager Chidi for a moment so we can get everything locked in.

I put everything you need to know right here—our itinerary, costs, payment dates, what I'm covering, and a few important details.

❄️

Also, because December weather likes to mind its own business and then randomly choose violence, I've added travel insurance/refundable options everywhere I could.

A Few Things to Know 💕

We are going to be eating GOOD this weekend.

For our meals and outings, everyone can plan to cover whatever food and drinks they personally order.

There are also a few things throughout the weekend that I'll be treating everyone to because you're coming to celebrate with me. 💕

Let the church say amen

We are NOT doing the "let's just split the bill evenly" ministry.

If one person ordered a side salad and a Diet Coke, she is not sponsoring somebody else's surf & turf and three margaritas.

i

Everyone has different appetites, preferences, and budgets, and I want everyone to be able to enjoy themselves without feeling weird about money.

ii

So please keep track of what you order.

iii

If a restaurant only allows one check, when that check comes, you should already know approximately what you spent.

The only math we should be doing together is tax and gratuity.
